1 Computer Vision & Biomechanical Posture Kinematics

Coordinates $(x, y) \in [0.0, 1.0]$ are normalized to the CameraX preview frame. In MediaPipe, $y=0$ is the image top and $y=1$ is the bottom. In an upright posture, the ear is higher than the shoulder ($y_{\text{ear}} < y_{\text{sh}}$).

1.1 Profile Coordinate Displacement
$$\Delta y = y_{\text{shoulder}} - y_{\text{ear}}, \qquad \Delta x = |x_{\text{ear}} - x_{\text{shoulder}}|$$
$\Delta y$ represents vertical elevation; $\Delta x$ represents forward horizontal translation. In upright posture, $\Delta y > 0$ strictly.
1.2 Head Droop / Severe Slouch Rejection Gate
$$\text{Slouch Condition} \iff \Delta y \le 0.01$$
If the ear drops level with or beneath the shoulder ($y_{\text{ear}} \ge y_{\text{sh}} - 0.01$), the posture is immediately gated as bad ($\theta_{\text{CVA}} = 0^\circ$).
1.3 Derived C7 (Neck Base) Anatomical Estimation
$$x_{\text{sh\_mid}} = \frac{x_{\text{shL}} + x_{\text{shR}}}{2}, \qquad y_{\text{sh\_mid}} = \frac{y_{\text{shL}} + y_{\text{shR}}}{2}$$ $$\Delta Y_{\text{up}} = \begin{cases} 0.11 \cdot H_{\text{torso}} & \text{if hips visible } (10\% - 15\% \text{ torso height}) \\ 0.22 \cdot H_{\text{neck}} & \text{if seated / no hips} \end{cases}$$ $$\Delta X_{\text{back}} = 0.12 \cdot H_{\text{neck}}, \qquad \operatorname{dir}_{\text{post}} = \begin{cases} +1 & \text{if facing left } (x_{\text{nose}} < x_{\text{ear}}) \\ -1 & \text{if facing right } (x_{\text{nose}} > x_{\text{ear}}) \end{cases}$$ $$y_{\text{C7}} = y_{\text{sh\_mid}} - \Delta Y_{\text{up}}, \qquad x_{\text{C7}} = x_{\text{sh\_mid}} + \big(\operatorname{dir}_{\text{post}} \cdot \Delta X_{\text{back}}\big)$$ $$\Delta X_{\text{px}} = |X_{\text{tragus, px}} - X_{\text{C7, px}}|, \qquad \Delta Y_{\text{px}} = Y_{\text{C7, px}} - Y_{\text{tragus, px}}$$
MediaPipe's shoulder landmarks (11 & 12) identify the outer acromion / glenohumeral joints, which sit lower and more anterior than the C7 vertebra (cervical spine base). C7 is derived from the shoulder midpoint by elevating upward toward the head ($10\% - 15\%$ of torso height or $22\%$ of neck height) and shifting backward along the dorsal neck column ($12\%$ of neck height away from the face).
1.4 Craniovertebral Angle (CVA / Pitch)
$$\theta_{\text{CVA}} = \operatorname{atan2}(\Delta Y_{\text{px}}, \Delta X_{\text{px}}) \times \frac{180^\circ}{\pi}, \qquad \theta_{\text{vert}} = 90^\circ - \theta_{\text{CVA}}$$
Calculates the true anatomical angle formed between the line from C7 (neck base) to tragus and the horizontal reference plane through C7.
1.4 Clinical Classification Rule
$$\text{Posture}(\theta_{\text{CVA}}, \Delta x) = \begin{cases} \text{"Good"} & \text{if } 48.0^\circ \le \theta_{\text{CVA}} \le 50.0^\circ \;\land\; \Delta x \le 0.16 \\ \text{"Poor"} & \text{if } (\theta_{\text{CVA}} < 48.0^\circ \;\lor\; \theta_{\text{CVA}} > 50.0^\circ) \;\lor\; \Delta x > 0.16 \end{cases}$$
$\theta_{\text{CVA}} \in [48.0^\circ, 50.0^\circ]$ is the validated clinical target range for neutral upright cervical alignment. Angles $< 48.0^\circ$ indicate Forward Head Posture (FHP), while angles $> 50.0^\circ$ indicate cervical hyperextension (head tilted back). $\Delta x \le 0.16$ prevents excessive anterior translation.
1.5 Lateral Head Roll (Coronal Plane Tilt)
$$\Delta x_{\text{ears}} = |x_{\text{ear}, 2} - x_{\text{ear}, 1}|, \qquad \Delta y_{\text{ears}} = y_{\text{ear}, 2} - y_{\text{ear}, 1}$$ $$\theta_{\text{roll}} = \operatorname{atan2}(\Delta y_{\text{ears}}, \Delta x_{\text{ears}}) \times \frac{180^\circ}{\pi} \quad \Big(|\theta_{\text{roll}}| \le 15.0^\circ\Big)$$
Evaluated when both ears are visible ($V_{\text{otherEar}} \ge 0.35$ and $\Delta x_{\text{ears}} > 0.01$). Flags lateral sideways head tilt.
1.6 Profile Side Selection & Landmark Quality Cutoff
$$V_{\text{left}} = \frac{V_{\text{ear}, L} + V_{\text{sh}, L}}{2}, \qquad V_{\text{right}} = \frac{V_{\text{ear}, R} + V_{\text{sh}, R}}{2}$$ $$\text{Selected Side} = \begin{cases} \text{Left Side} & \text{if } V_{\text{left}} \ge V_{\text{right}} \\ \text{Right Side} & \text{if } V_{\text{right}} > V_{\text{left}} \end{cases} \quad \Big(V_{\text{ear}} \ge 0.30 \;\land\; V_{\text{sh}} \ge 0.30\Big)$$
1.7 Composite Posture Verification Function
$$\text{isCorrect} = \mathbb{I}\Big( (48.0^\circ \le \theta_{\text{CVA}} \le 50.0^\circ) \;\land\; (\Delta x \le 0.16) \;\land\; (|\theta_{\text{roll}}| \le 15^\circ) \;\land\; (|x_{\text{midShoulder}} - x_{\text{midHip}}| \le 0.08) \Big)$$

2 Wearable Hardware (ESP32) & IMU Sensor Calibration

Formulas for 3-axis accelerometer gravity decomposition and baseline neutral tilt calibration at 50 Hz.

2.1 Accelerometer Gravity Vector Decomposition
$$a_x = -\sin(\theta_{\text{pitch}}), \quad a_y = \cos(\theta_{\text{pitch}}) \cdot \sin(\theta_{\text{roll}}), \quad a_z = \cos(\theta_{\text{pitch}}) \cdot \cos(\theta_{\text{roll}})$$
Aerospace standard gravity decomposition representing body orientation with normalized gravity magnitude $\|\vec{g}\| = 1.0$.
2.2 Low-Pass Filtered Gravity Mean ($N = 250$ Samples)
$$N = \left\lfloor \frac{T}{1000 / f_s} \right\rfloor = \frac{5000\text{ ms}}{20\text{ ms}} = 250\text{ samples}$$ $$\bar{a}_x = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N a_{x, i}, \qquad \bar{a}_y = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N a_{y, i}, \qquad \bar{a}_z = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N a_{z, i}$$
2.3 Pitch and Roll Angle Extraction from Accelerometer
$$\theta_{\text{pitch}} = \operatorname{atan2}\left(-\bar{a}_x, \sqrt{\bar{a}_y^2 + \bar{a}_z^2}\right) \times \frac{180^\circ}{\pi}, \qquad \theta_{\text{roll}} = \operatorname{atan2}\left(\bar{a}_y, \bar{a}_z\right) \times \frac{180^\circ}{\pi}$$
Derives neutral physical pitch and roll angles from the averaged static gravity vector.
2.4 Hardware Baseline Sensor Acceptance Boundaries
$$\text{Valid Sensor Calibration} \iff \Big( -25.0^\circ \le \theta_{\text{sensorPitch}} \le 25.0^\circ \Big) \;\land\; \Big( |\theta_{\text{sensorRoll}}| \le 15.0^\circ \Big)$$
2.5 Temporal Stability Hold Window
$$t_{\text{stable}} = \frac{N_{\text{stable\_frames}}}{\text{FPS}} = \frac{30\text{ frames}}{30\text{ fps}} = 1.0\text{ second}$$

3 Analytics, Posture Scoring & Tracking Models

3.1 Discrete Posture Score Mapping
$$\mathcal{S}(p) = \begin{cases} 95 & \text{if } p = \text{"Excellent"} \\ 85 & \text{if } p = \text{"Good"} \\ 70 & \text{if } p = \text{"Okay"} \\ 50 & \text{if } p = \text{"Poor"} \\ 30 & \text{if } p = \text{"Very poor"} \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
3.2 Good Posture Compliance Percentage
$$\%_{\text{Good}} = \left( \frac{\sum_{i=1}^N \mathbb{I}\big(p_i \in \{\text{"Excellent"}, \text{"Good"}\}\big)}{N} \right) \times 100$$
3.3 Categorical Frequency Distribution Percentage
$$\%_{c} = \left( \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \mathbb{I}(p_i = c) \right) \times 100, \quad c \in \{\text{"Excellent"}, \text{"Good"}, \text{"Okay"}, \text{"Poor"}\}$$
3.4 Daily Average & Hourly Posture Quality Scores
$$\bar{S}_{\text{day}} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \mathcal{S}(p_i), \qquad \bar{S}_h = \frac{1}{|K_h|} \sum_{i \in K_h} \mathcal{S}(p_i) \quad (K_h = \{i \mid \operatorname{hour}(t_i) = h\})$$
3.5 Active Time Tracked (Minutes)
$$T_{\text{active}} = \max\left(1, \; \left\lfloor \frac{\max(t) - \min(t)}{60} \right\rfloor\right) \quad (\text{timestamps } t \text{ in Unix seconds})$$

4 UI Canvas Geometry & Gauge Trigonometry

4.1 Radial Posture Gauge Sweep Arc
$$\theta_{\text{start}} = 135.0^\circ, \qquad \theta_{\text{sweep}} = 270.0^\circ \times \left( \frac{\mathcal{S}}{100} \right)$$
A $270^\circ$ circular arc drawn with rounded stroke caps. Starts at bottom-left ($135^\circ$) and sweeps clockwise toward bottom-right.
4.2 Gauge Indicator Needle Knob Coordinates
$$\theta_{\text{rad}} = \left(135^\circ + 270^\circ \times \frac{\mathcal{S}}{100}\right) \times \frac{\pi}{180^\circ}$$ $$X_{\text{knob}} = X_{\text{center}} + R \cdot \cos(\theta_{\text{rad}}), \qquad Y_{\text{knob}} = Y_{\text{center}} + R \cdot \sin(\theta_{\text{rad}})$$
Computes the precise $(X, Y)$ Canvas position of the glowing indicator knob along perimeter radius $R = \frac{\min(W, H)}{2}$.
4.3 Dynamic Canvas Bar Chart Geometry
$$W_{\text{slot}} = \frac{W_{\text{chart}}}{M}, \qquad W_{\text{bar}} = \min\big(0.48 \cdot W_{\text{slot}}, \; 34\text{ dp}\big)$$ $$H_{\text{bar}} = \left(\frac{\bar{S}_h}{100}\right) \times (H_{\text{chart}} - 28\text{ dp}) \times p_{\text{anim}}$$ $$X_{\text{bar}, i} = i \cdot W_{\text{slot}} + \frac{W_{\text{slot}} - W_{\text{bar}}}{2}, \qquad Y_{\text{bar}, i} = H_{\text{chart}} - H_{\text{bar}}$$
Positions and sizes rounded-corner score bars along the timeline axis with dynamic interpolation factor $p_{\text{anim}} \in [0.0, 1.0]$.

5 System & Battery Telemetry

5.1 Hardware Device Battery Telemetry Parsing & Clamping
$$\text{Raw Pct} = \operatorname{parse}\big(\text{deviceBattery} \lor \text{battery} \lor \text{batteryLevel}\big), \qquad \text{Battery Pct} = \min\Big(100, \; \max(0, \; \text{Raw Pct})\Big)$$
Parses real-time hardware telemetry synced directly from the wearable device to Firebase Firestore (posture_data, esp32/status) and clamps to the valid integer range $[0, 100]\%$.
5.2 Low Battery Boolean Trigger
$$\text{isLowBattery} = \mathbb{I}(\text{Battery Pct} < 20)$$
Triggers warning amber/coral alerts when wearable device battery falls below $20\%$.
